🌿 How to Use
• Follow the directions provided on the product label.
• Apply an appropriate amount as needed.
• Use as part of your daily personal care routine.
• Rinse thoroughly if required by the product.
• For best results, use consistently as directed.

📦 Storage
• Store in a cool, dry place.
• Keep away from direct sunlight and excessive heat.
• Keep the product container tightly closed when not in use.
• Store out of reach of children.

⚠️ Caution
• For external use only.
• Avoid contact with eyes unless the product is specifically intended for eye use.
• If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
• Keep out of reach of children.
